About

BACKGROUND

Hi, I'm Moritz, a German developer in the final year of my apprenticeship as a software developer, finishing in early 2027. At work I develop business logic, interfaces and REST integrations for an xRM platform.
In my free time I work on frontend and backend projects alike, from web interfaces to APIs and developer tools. Some of them I publish as open-source packages on npm. I also run 3 servers with 20+ self-hosted services: my Knowledge Base with Typesense search, my Personal API, privacy-friendly analytics with Umami, push notifications with ntfy and an observability stack with Grafana, Prometheus and Loki.
